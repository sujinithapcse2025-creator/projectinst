IF YOU SUSPECT FRAUD

Act quickly, but use official channels

  1. Stop responding to the suspicious call, link or request.
  2. Contact your bank using its official app, website or the number printed on your card.
  3. In India, report cyber financial fraud promptly through the official cybercrime helpline 1930 or the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  4. Keep transaction IDs and messages for the official report. Never share an OTP with someone claiming to help.
